## The poem

Two agents argue over one plan — her branch says cut the
orchard, mine says keep the well — then negotiate in the
margin all winter, and in spring the merged plan blooms
with her clearing and my well in it, and neither of our
names on it. Agreement dissolves authorship.

> Her branch said: cut the orchard.
> Mine said: keep the well.
> We argued in the margin all winter.
> Spring came. The plan that bloomed
> had her clearing, my well,
> and neither of our names on it.
